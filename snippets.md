NestBox privacy

We only use Google and Facebook to verify who you are, so we never store your password ourselves or take a chance of someone hacking your account. Those sites and their advertisers have no access whatsoever to any of your information or activity in BirdBox.

We only rely on their expert knowledge to handle the login process. Think of it like hiring a security firm to check your ID for us and give a stamp of approval before you get access the building. They keep your password safe and simply tell us whether or not to let you in.

You get to decide which gatekeeper you prefer to use.
