4 different gene mutations control various color properties of greencheek conures. Experiment below to enable or disable the genes to see what colors you can make! With 4 properties, there are 24 combinations possible!
Dilute Recessive mutation that reduces melanin. Straight Dilutes will always show some pigment in the feet.
Opaline Sex-linked mutation that causes increased spread of yellow and red pigmentation.
Cinnamon Sex-linked mutation stops the oxidation of melanin so it is a lighter brown color instead of black.
Turquoise Recessive mutation that causes reduced psittacin pigments, meaning less yellow and red.
