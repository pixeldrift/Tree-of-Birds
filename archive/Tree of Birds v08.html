Data Format Guide

Upload a CSV or TSV file that includes the following column headers:

id, name, sex, motherID, fatherID

Notes:

  • ID numbers must be unique, may be zero padded.
  • Sex should be M or F.
  • Parents (motherID, fatherID) refer to other IDs in the same file.

Example:

idnamesexmotherIDfatherID
001BluejayF
002RobinM
003SparrowF001002
004FinchM001002
